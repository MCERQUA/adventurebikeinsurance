## The insurance-relevant contrasts (this is the article's spine)
| | 890 R | T7 | Ibex 800 E |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front wheel | 21" spoked | 21" spoked | **19" cast/tubeless** |
| Travel f/r | 240/240 mm | 211/201 mm | **160/150 mm** |
| Wet weight | 474 lb | 459 lb | **509 lb** |
| Warranty | — | **1 yr** | **2 yr** |
| Farkles | aftermarket | aftermarket | **factory-fitted** |

Three consequences a standard policy handles badly:
1. Two of the three are built to leave pavement; the off-road/off-pavement question is not academic.
2. The 890 R and T7 get their protection bolted on afterwards (aftermarket = "accessories", capped);
   the Ibex arrives with its bars and skid plate as OEM (not "accessories" — valued differently).
3. Tall seats + loaded bikes = low-speed tip-overs, the most common ADV claim and the one riders
   most often eat themselves because the deductible exceeds the plastic.

## Misconceptions to correct
- "Full coverage" covers off-road. It frequently does not.
- Accessory coverage is unlimited. Default is typically $1,000–$3,000.
- Roadside assistance will reach you. Standard tow limits start around 15 miles.
- A cheaper bike is cheaper to insure. Displacement/class/parts cost drive it, not sticker price.
